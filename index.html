Definitions and notes
  • LLM hit: the LLM rated the comment 4 or higher on its category. The tables also try 5 and 6.
  • Spearman: how closely the human numbers follow the LLM's ranking across comments. 1 is a perfect match, 0 is none.
  • Kappa: agreement between raters on Agree/Disagree beyond what chance gives. About half of all answers were Agree, so a lot of agreement is expected by chance.
  • ICC: the share of variation in answers that comes from differences between comments rather than between raters. One-way random effects, for unequal numbers of raters per comment. For Agree/Disagree, Agree is 1 and Disagree is 0.
  • 3-rater consensus: the average, over random panels of three raters per comment, of the correlation with the LLM.
  • Majority: more than half of a comment's raters. With an even split there is no majority, so the comment counts as not endorsed.
  • Sampling: comments were drawn in bands of the LLM's rating within each category, so the data contain more high and low ratings than the full corpus. These are checks of agreement, not estimates of how common a category is.
  • Raters: different people took each study. Raters who failed the attention check were excluded (one in the 1–7 study, none in Agree/Disagree). Faster-than-expected raters were flagged and kept.
  • Pilot: an earlier Agree/Disagree run on 30 different comments, with 30 raters on each.
