Definitions and notes
  • LLM hit: the LLM rated the comment 4 or higher on its category. The tables also try 5 and 6.
  • Spearman: how closely the human numbers follow the LLM's ranking across comments. 1 is a perfect match, 0 is none.
  • Kappa: agreement between raters on Agree/Disagree beyond what chance gives. About half of all answers were Agree, so a lot of agreement is expected by chance.
  • ICC: the share of variation in 1–7 ratings that comes from differences between comments rather than between raters. One-way random effects, for unequal numbers of raters per comment.
  • 3-rater consensus: the average, over random panels of three raters per comment, of the correlation with the LLM.
  • Sampling: comments were drawn in bands of the LLM's rating within each category, so both studies contain more high and low ratings than the full corpus. These are checks of agreement, not estimates of how common a category is.
  • Exclusion: one rater in the rating study failed the attention check. One more was flagged as fast but kept.
