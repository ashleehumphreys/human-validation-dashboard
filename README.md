# Human validation dashboard

Results of human-validation studies of an LLM's 1-7 ratings of Reddit comments on six categories of how people talk about generative AI:

- **Head-to-head**: the same 180 comments rated in two formats by different raters.
- **Agree/Disagree**: 30 raters, 180 comments, 2-4 raters per comment.
- **1-7 rating**: 32 raters, 180 comments, 3-4 raters per comment.
- **Pilot**: an earlier Agree/Disagree run, 30 raters on 30 other comments.

Open `index.html` (it loads d3 from cdnjs). The page contains aggregate numbers only: no comment text and no rater identifiers.
Snapshot of the 13 and 19 November 2026 Qualtrics exports.
